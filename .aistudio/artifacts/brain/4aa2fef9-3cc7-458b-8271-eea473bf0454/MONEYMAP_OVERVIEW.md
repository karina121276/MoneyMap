# MoneyMap Application Overview

MoneyMap is a premium, offline-first personal finance application designed for users who want complete control over their financial data without relying on cloud services or mandatory accounts.

## Core Information
- **Name**: MoneyMap
- **Package ID**: `com.thecorporatedeveloper.moneymap`
- **Version**: 1.0.1 (Code: 1)
- **Developer**: The Corporate Developer
- **Category**: Finance
- **Purpose**: Offline-first personal expense and income management.
- **Minimum Android Version**: Android 7.0 (API 24)
- **Target Android Version**: Android 16 (API 36)
- **Languages**: English (Default)
- **Currencies**: Indian Rupee (₹), US Dollar ($), Euro (€), British Pound (£)
- **Internet**: Not required for core functionality. Only needed for external link navigation (YouTube, Privacy Policy) and App Check.
- **Backend/Cloud**: None.
- **Advertising**: None (No SDKs included).
- **Storage**: Local SQLite (Room) database.

## Description
MoneyMap empowers you to track expenses, manage income, plan budgets, and visualize your financial health entirely on your device, ensuring total privacy and data sovereignty.

## Strongest Selling Points
1. **100% Offline-First**: Your data never leaves your device.
2. **No Mandatory Registration**: Start tracking immediately, no accounts needed.
3. **Complete Data Control**: Full JSON backup and restore capabilities.
4. **Smart Entry & OCR**: Automatically capture transactions from screenshots and receipts.
5. **Budget Planning**: Set, track, and manage monthly spending limits.
6. **Detailed Reports**: Gain insights with visual breakdowns of income and expenses.
7. **Privacy-Focused**: No third-party tracking or advertising.
8. **Customizable Experience**: Themes, currency settings, and custom categories.
9. **Android Native**: Built with Jetpack Compose for a fast, modern UI.
10. **Receipt Attachments**: Store images of receipts directly with transactions.
