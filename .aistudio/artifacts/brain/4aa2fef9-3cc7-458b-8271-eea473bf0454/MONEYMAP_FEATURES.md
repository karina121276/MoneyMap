## Detailed Catalogue

### 1. Home Dashboard
- **What it does**: Displays overall financial summary, balance, recent transactions, and budget progress.
- **Why**: Quick overview of financial health.
- **Offline**: Yes.

### 2. Smart Entry & OCR
- **What it does**: Uses ML Kit to extract transaction details from payment screenshots.
- **How**: Share screenshot to MoneyMap.
- **Offline**: Yes, processing is local.

### 3. Backup & Restore
- **What it does**: Exports/imports full database to/from JSON or ZIP (with images).
- **Why**: Protect against data loss.
- **Offline**: Yes.

### 4. Budget Management
- **What it does**: Sets monthly limits per category or overall.
- **Why**: Keeps spending in check.
- **Offline**: Yes.
